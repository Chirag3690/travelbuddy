Vinle helps you find travel buddies and squads for events, trips, and open travel plans.

## Information we collect

- **Account:** name, email, profile photo (via Google sign-in)
- **Profile:** age, gender, location, bio, interests, travel destinations, photos you upload
- **Usage:** swipes, matches, squad membership, messages you send in chat
- **Device:** basic app diagnostics (crash logs via app store / Expo if enabled)

- Show your profile to other users in Discover (unless you turn on “Hide from discover”)

- Enable chat and squad features
- Improve the app
